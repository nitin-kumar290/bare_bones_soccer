About Bare Bones Soccer

Based out of Denver, Colorado — we're a community soccer collective on a mission to change how the game is experienced. We host pickup games, one-of-a-kind soccer events and social happenings that prove soccer is more than a sport — it's a cultural connector. From breweries to parking lots, fields to courts, we turn everyday spaces into vibrant soccer gatherings across the Denver metro. We're breaking the mold of standard soccer culture — building something social, creative and rooted in shared experience. Take an inside look at how the world's game brings people together, one pitch, party and parallel at a time. By soccer people, for soccer people.

Who are we?

Bare Bones Soccer Collective is a community-based soccer organization based in the Denver Metro Area. Stripping the game down to its purest form, Bare Bones Soccer is built for those who just want to play, connect and enjoy the sport without hurdles. From free weekly pickup to unique social games at breweries and local venues, it's all about bringing people together through soccer. No egos, no rules — just the game, the culture and the community.
