FAQ

Good Questions

I don't have a full team — can I still play?

Registration is team-based, so you'll need a squad of 4–6 (including at least 1 female player) before you register.

What if it rains?

Games go on unless conditions are unsafe — we'll post any changes to the WhatsApp group and Instagram.

What's your refund policy?

No refunds once the season starts, unless the league itself is disbanded.

Do we need to bring anything?

Cleats or turf shoes and a light/dark shirt for color scrimmages — we'll cover the rest.
