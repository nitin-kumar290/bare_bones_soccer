⚽ Anyone looking for a fun league? Found something different.

Just came across this and had to share — a 4v4 street soccer league happening at Waldschänke Ciders, right in a parking lot under the lights. Not your typical rec league setup, feels way more like a pickup game with actual stakes, plus you can grab a cider after.

📍 Waldschänke Ciders — 4100 Jason St, Denver
🗓️ Wednesday nights, starting 9/9
⏰ 6:30–8:30 PM
🔥 7–8 week season, coed, 18+

Don't need a full team either — bring a squad, split one with friends, or sign up solo as a free agent and they'll place you on a roster with an open spot.

$50/player on your own, or $40/player if a captain wants to prepay the whole team.

Details and sign-up here: https://barebonessoccer.com/leagues/waldy-street-league/
