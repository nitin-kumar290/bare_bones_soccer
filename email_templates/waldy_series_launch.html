🏡 Robin's Homebuying Tip of the Month
Shop homeowners insurance early. Colorado homeowners insurance has increased over 40% in the last 4 years and continues to rise — driven by the Louisville/Superior fires in 2021 and ongoing wind and hail claims. If the roof on a home you're considering is over 10 years old, your insurance premium could be as much as 50% higher. Know the insurance costs for your target area and home style before you fall in love with a listing.
